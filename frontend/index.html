Key Theoretical Takeaways

  • Smoothness Paradox: Standard MSE optimizes conditional mean, penalizing large errors. This suppresses peak variances and makes MSE models completely blind to ramps at label threshold (F1 = 0.4%).
  • Ramp-Aware Objective: Adding an explicit F1 loss term φ = w1·MSE + w2·(1 - F1) yields high ramp recall (up to 55%), trading off ~20% level RMSE.
  • Lag History Impact: Increasing history from Lag 1 to Lag 6 is the single largest factor in restoring direction accuracy (57.1% → 66.3%).
  • Warm-Start Superiority: In high-dimensional optimization (d = 1608), random PSO starts stagnate. Seeding particles from an Adam solution achieves peak test AP of 0.290.
